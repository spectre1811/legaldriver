Peshi — round 2 · researched concepts

Grounded in two findings: (1) the शिरोरेखा — the horizontal headline stroke every Devanagari letter hangs from, and the script's single most recognisable feature — is the *same gesture* as our brass hearing-date rule; and (2) peshi literally means an appearance / attendance before the court. So the strongest ideas make one brass line do bilingual double-duty, or speak to "showing up."

The core move (01–02): the brass rule sits where a Devanagari reader expects the शिरोरेखा — so the identical brass line reads as "Devanagari headline" AND "hearing-date underline" at once. Script-native, product-true, and ownable in a way a glyph-in-a-box isn't.
