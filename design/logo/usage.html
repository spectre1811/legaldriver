Do & don’t

DoUse the dark tile on light surfaces, the light tile on dark. Keep the brass underline. Give it clearspace. Use the supplied favicon/PWA PNGs for icons.
Don’tRecolour outside ink/brass/paper. Stretch, rotate, add shadows/gradients/glow. Swap the font. Place on a busy photo. Re-type the “p” in a different serif.
